Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our treatments.

General Questions

What skin types do you treat?

We treat all skin types, including sensitive, acne-prone, dry, and aging skin. Our aestheticians will customize a plan for you.

Are your products organic?

We offer a specialized range of organic treatments. We also utilize clinically proven, science-backed formulas depending on your skin's needs.

Booking & Policies

How do I book an appointment?

You can book directly through our contact page, via phone, or by visiting our clinic in person.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require a 24-hour notice for any cancellations. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a 50% charge of the treatment fee.

Preparing for Your Visit

Should I wear makeup to my appointment?

We recommend coming with a bare face if possible, but our aestheticians will always perform a double cleanse to start any facial treatment.

How long will the results last?

Results vary depending on the treatment and your home care routine. Most facial glows last 1-2 weeks, while advanced treatments offer longer-lasting results.
